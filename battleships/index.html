Sink the enemy fleet before they sink yours.

Open a game, send the link to a friend, and take turns firing across a 10×10 chart. Ships stay hidden on the server, so nobody can peek.

Fleet

  • Carrier5 cells
  • Battleship4 cells
  • Cruiser3 cells
  • Submarine3 cells
  • Destroyer2 cells

Rules

  1. Place the five ships across or down. They may not overlap or touch, not even at a corner.
  2. Take turns firing. A hit earns another shot, a miss passes the turn.
  3. The first to sink the whole enemy fleet wins.
